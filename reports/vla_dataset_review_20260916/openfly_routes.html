This is recorded-route replay with offline predictions. The camera follows the dataset route. Model actions are shown alongside it; they do not drive this replay. A wrong label is not automatically a failed flight. The published flight-success test allows stopping within 20 m of the goal.

Confirmed: the decoder changed the apparent result

Campus packed TRAIN episode: correct source decoder → 12/15 directions, 15/15 valid. Wrong vertical decoder, SAME tokens → 0/15 valid. Constant vertical dimensions were normalized differently during training. The last repair used a vertical-capable profile too broadly. Both profiles are now shown explicitly; exact source calibration is established for the packed records, while raw-route profile assignments remain provisional.

The altitude packed TRAIN episode still scores only 7/19 directions with its verified source decoder. The training prompt/pooling control improves this to 10/19. This repair does not explain every error.

Additional data discrepancies

The authors’ packed training episodes contain future images in history: 1 of 15 steps in the campus sample and 3 of 19 in the altitude sample. The first-step removal in their training pipeline does not remove these affected steps. Exact stored inputs below deliberately reproduce that issue as a diagnostic; the causal control replaces only future images. Removing these future images changed no generated tokens in either episode, so the defect did not explain these sampled predictions.

Published flight success: 33.2% seen / 10.7% unseen. Their oracle success (ever reaching within 20 m) is 63.5% / 48.9%. Our next-action agreement is a different metric. No claim that Qwen is the better flight policy.
